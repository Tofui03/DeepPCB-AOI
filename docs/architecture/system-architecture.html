DeepPCB-AOI • System Architecture

Three-Channel Reference Fusion & Deterministic Guardrail Pipeline

Bare-board optical inspection pairing 3-channel composite inputs ([template, test, absdiff]) with an ONNX Runtime YOLOv8 detector (0.9401 F1, 9.95 ms GPU) and deterministic IPC-A-600 area & batch line-halt guardrails.

1. Three-Channel Fusion

Why [Template, Test, |Δ|]

Single-image detectors cannot tell intentional drill vias from pin-holes (74.0% precision) or close traces from copper shorts. Passing the template in Channel 0 and absolute difference in Channel 2 boosts Pin-hole F1 from 0.8507 to 0.9631 (+11.2%) and Short F1 from 0.8580 to 0.9273 (+6.9%).

2. Sub-10ms GPU Inference

Takt-Time & Fast-Path

The 3-channel YOLOv8n ONNX detector runs in 9.95 ms mean latency on an RTX 5060 Laptop GPU (34.13 ms on an Intel Core Ultra 7 255HX CPU). Defect-free boards take the 0-box fast-path directly to PASS without invoking downstream stages.

3. Deterministic Guardrails

Zero-Trust Safety Overrides

Open and Short defects are forced to SCRAP / CRITICAL (0 px tolerance). Non-fatal classes check pixel-area caps, and the third occurrence of any defect class in a batch sets halt_production_line = True in SQLite.
